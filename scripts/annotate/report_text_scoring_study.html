Text scoring: identity, paraphrases, and material differences

The viewer displays raw off-the-shelf model predictions. An exact-match override was tested and rejected; it remains a labeled diagnostic in this report. STS predictions are not percentages of semantic agreement.

Frozen comparison: 33 authored controls and 33 current Sol/Astra description pairs. Five local scorers and two independent Gemini judges; judges see anonymous A/B text in both input orders. Real pairs have no human reference labels.

Decision: keep STS as a clearly labeled raw baseline while investigating a better scorer. NLI and PAWS are promising on explicit contradiction controls; neither has yet established reliable contextual annotation agreement. Independent judges expose matched claims, material differences, contradictions, and unresolved identities. These experiments do not establish a single calibrated acceptance score.

Local scorer diagnostics

Higher equivalence scores and lower contradiction scores are useful sanity checks. These columns use raw predictions. Self-score division can exceed 1 and does not repair semantic mistakes. NLI entailment probabilities and paraphrase probabilities are different quantities from STS or cosine.

Independent judge diagnostics

Scalar judges assign a direct 0–100 score plus a relation. Fact judges extract and align assertions; code computes 2 × equivalent fact matches / total facts. Slot judges compare nine fixed dimensions, with equivalent applicable dimensions / all applicable dimensions. Contradictions and unresolved correspondences remain separately visible. None is calibrated against human labels.

Research implications


G-Eval is an evaluation framework using an LLM and a rubric, rather than a separate trained semantic reasoner. Its original results concern summarization and dialogue, and do not validate robot annotation scoring. Using a 100-point scale instead of five bins does not establish calibration or stable precision.


LLM-Rubric learns calibration against human judgements across multiple dimensions. We cannot reproduce that validation without human labels. Prometheus 2 provides specialized open judge models and is another candidate; it was researched, not run in this CPU-only experiment.
